About Me

Hi, my name is Lacey Fox, and I am from Cleveland, Ohio. I go to Indiana University, where I am majoring in biology on the pre-med track. I chose biology because I want to become a dermatologist one day. I have always been interested in science and health, and I am excited to learn more about these subjects throughout college. I know becoming a dermatologist will take a lot of school and hard work, but it is something I really want to accomplish.

Some of my favorite things to do are working out, shopping, making art, and hanging out with my friends. I really enjoy workout classes, especially Pilates and VersaClimber classes. They are challenging, but they are also a fun way for me to stay active. When I am with my friends, we like going out to eat, trying new places, shopping, and getting ice cream together.

I have worked at a gymnastics facility and at the front desk of a gym. These jobs have helped me become more responsible, organized, and comfortable talking to people. I also like that both jobs involve fitness, since that is something I am interested in.

I like writing, especially when I can write about something that interests me. I prefer reading nonfiction because I like learning about real life things.
